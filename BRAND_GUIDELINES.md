# Brand Context: Cana Gardens

## 1. Brand Identity & Positioning
- **Brand Name:** Cana Gardens
- **Tagline:** "Your miracle begins here"
- **Core Purpose:** An accessible yet intimate event venue offering a pocket of serenity, quiet, and escape.
- **Tone of Voice:** Elegant, serene, tranquil, welcoming, intimate, and refined.
- **Writing Style:** Use calm, graceful language. Focus on themes of natural beauty, peace, celebration, and seamless hospitality. Avoid loud, aggressive, or overly commercial buzzwords.

## 2. Visual & Design Tokens (For UI/Frontend, CSS, or Design Tasks)
- **Primary Color:** `#356939` (Deep Green) | RGB(53, 105, 57) | CMYK(78%, 33%, 92%, 29%)
- **Secondary Color:** `#FFFFFF` (White) | RGB(255, 255, 255) | CMYK(0%, 0%, 0%, 0%)
- **Tertiary / Accent Color:** `#9DC234` (Vibrant Leaf Green) | RGB(157, 194, 30) | CMYK(39%, 0%, 100%, 6%)
- **Headings & Titles Font:** Playfair Display (Serif)
- **Subtitles & Body Font:** Montserrat (Sans-Serif)

## 3. Brand Imagery & Motifs
- **Logo Concept:** A stylized vine forming the letter "C", inspired by the flora of Cana.
- **Visual Aesthetic:** Organic botanicals, botanical line art, minimalist whitespace, natural textures, and garden serenity.

## 4. LLM Operational Instructions
- When drafting **copy** (web pages, social captions, brochures, emails), adhere strictly to the serene, elegant tone and the tagline.
- When generating **code/markup** (CSS, Tailwind, HTML, component styling), use the exact hex codes and typography defined above.
